## Final behavior and review

Both ordinary editor routes expose archive with explicit confirmation, preserving an unsaved draft on cancellation. Management opens an actual owned archived-cashflow list with restore/delete, original art, signed exact source amounts and dates, loading/error/retry and live updates. Restoring validates current dependencies, exact cash/card availability, paid statements and duplicate same-month card payments. Actions preserve historical raw/unknown/creator/version fields and support complete known-result no-write replay after ACK.

One seven-entity Room transaction recomputes and commits the primary, canonical investment postings/automatic adjustments/tombstones and outbox, checking full source/owner/caller cancellation before success. Manual allocations and unrelated queues remain protected. Archive/restore keep receipts. Permanent deletion conditionally removes only the original owned image after a complete financial tombstone proof; lost responses, real cancellation, owner/DI replacement and concurrent newer images are covered. Post-commit cleanup failure is typed and disclosed: Retry keeps the frozen original snapshot; explicit Keep receipt knowingly accepts retention. Outside/back dismissal cannot silently lose this state. Durable process-kill garbage collection is not delivered here.

Android maintains the exact-money requirement by updating derived effects atomically where Swift currently saves only a lifecycle row. Fresh scoped reviewer found no Critical findings, one Important misleading receipt-cleanup outcome and one Minor long-title scrolling defect. Both were corrected with observed native RED→GREEN. Additional legacy missing/lowercase currency regressions were observed RED→GREEN; restoration validates normalized currency declarations while preserving raw spelling/omissions.
