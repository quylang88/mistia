## Behavior and review

The client fetches by exact owner/id, creates with return=representation and conditionally updates by owner/id/expected Long version. Explicit nulls and opaque fields survive. Only a minimal owner/version/device/date tombstone patch may clear a deleted channel; deleted creation, other entities, forged identities, malformed or ambiguous responses are rejected. Reads remain possible when write gates are false. The captured exact AuthSession, caller and dynamic gate fence request/results; body cancellation cancels the real OkHttp call, redirects are refused, body reads are bounded to2MB and failures retain status codes without body/token/URL disclosure. HTTPS tests use MockWebServer only.

The coordinator uses the established conflict engine for semantic ACK, missing-row create, known-base update, create/conditional races, local/remote winners, unresolved conflict and bounded retry. It checks an atomic Room snapshot of row, dirty marker and exact queue before remote work and again before a write. Typed acknowledgement/failure methods run under an outer Room transaction, binding owner/id/entity/payload/kind/base/version/date/device stamp and complete queued state. They preserve newer queues, unqueued row changes, detached dirty/queue state and device-column mismatches. Final active-owner and original-caller checks roll back even an applied ACK/queue deletion or failure UPDATE. No generic writer fallback is allowed.

Direct Swift review used SupabaseRemoteStore181–182/255–256 (channel CRUD), the asset RPC/upload sequence183+ as the next dependency and RemoteInvestmentChannel in InvestmentSyncModels.swift3+ (Date/Int64 wire fields). Shared existing conflict behavior compares the remote base while advancing beyond max(local,remote), so the client permits a strictly higher exact Long instead of wrongly requiring base+1. Equivalent archived timestamps such as Z and .000+00:00 compare as the same instant for semantic ACK and visible consent; the original wire representation is retained. A genuinely different instant remains a change. Author review also found and fixed the missing queue-device versus payload-device coherence check.
