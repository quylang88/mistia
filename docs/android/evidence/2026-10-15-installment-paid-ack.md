# Installment paid-history ACK without remote writes — 2026-10-15

Branch `codex/android-apk2-4-parity`; baseline `4b5fea99`.

## Completed scope

Added an ACK-only worker for owned installment paid occurrence UPSERT/DELETE queues. It never calls remote create/conditionalUpdate and adds no paid-write capability to the network client. It is composed inside the single due-domain coordinator, independent of the bill delegate and alongside the distinct source-delete/skip workers. App readiness requires global+due+installment+wallet+transaction+category flags; all remain false.

The worker captures complete owner plan/due/ledger/wallet records, all corresponding queue retry/observed metadata and the exact causal batch. Source-delete batches and pending source/ledger dependencies defer. It reads the owned authoritative plan, fully paged source/month collection, exact linked expense and wallet. Parent business drift waits for pull; metadata-only ACKs preserve the creation-time precision tolerance. Existing archived source/wallet presentation can retain paid history; deleted sources/wallets and unsupported card/investment wallet states hold. Exact positive posted expense, frozen occurrence amount/link/time presence, same currency and local/remote financial footprint must agree before an existing paid server record can ACK. Description/metadata-only expense updates do not invent a financial conflict. Missing remote paid records remain queued for the still-unimplemented write path.

Completed undo may ACK an absent or already-tombstoned occurrence only after its linked expense is absent/tombstoned locally and authoritatively. If expense DELETE lost and normal pull/transaction ACK restored a live matching expense, an authoritative paid occurrence can be restored atomically too. Changed or not-yet-pulled financial dependencies defer rather than rewriting a ledger. Legacy nil-link paid tombstones can ACK absence without inventing an expense; new/live nil-link paid repair remains an explicit conflict. Different financial sibling records hold for reconciliation. Identical live paid clones may preserve an active payment; a live paid sibling during undo holds for complete child reconciliation, even if it shares the expense.

Room recomputes the pure decision, rechecks the entire raw snapshot/current owner, removes only the exact queue and saves the authoritative occurrence in one transaction. It never updates the source, ledger or wallet. Snapshot-scoped failure persistence cannot label or clear a newer queue, source/financial edit or causal batch. Cancellation and persistence failures propagate without a generic fallback. Absent-server undo ACK preserves the original raw payload, including unknown fields, explicit nulls and exact Long values. Remote numeric wallet version/expense amount are validated as numbers, not quoted strings.
