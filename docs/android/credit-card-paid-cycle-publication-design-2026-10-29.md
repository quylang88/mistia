A paid marker is historical evidence linking an existing posted internal transfer to a selected statement month and scheduled date. Today's profile linked wallet, closing/due days, archive state and current charge aggregate can change after a legitimate payment. Publishing the existing marker must not reprice that transfer, infer FX, silently relabel it or require today's configured wallet/schedule to equal the frozen history. Statement views independently recompute current charges and only reuse matching marker amounts; they can use a posted transfer that covers the current amount. Tests must prove metadata/charge edits preserve marker facts, rather than changing those facts to make today's calculation agree.

For active writes require a live owned ordinary posted `transfer/internalTransfer`, source wallet owned/normal/non-system, destination owned creditCard, all three currencies equal valid codes, effective destination amount equal source amount (no hidden FX), positive marker amount at most the credited amount, marker paidAt equal payment occurredAt, and exact immutable ledger/cycle births/IDs. This accepts a legacy overpayment covering a smaller statement amount while the new167 flow remains exact full payment. Archived source/card rows may describe valid already-posted history; deleted/missing rows cannot authorize a new active marker. Current profile selection/schedule is not mutation authority and is not rewritten. A later metadata change is not permission to create new money.

A tombstone may publish only after the actual linked payment is deleted/absent and its retained local identity still proves the old financial footprint.
