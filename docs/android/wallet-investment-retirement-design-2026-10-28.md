The current wallet-first engine may attempt that archive too early; the investment publisher also requires clean active wallet dependencies. Runtime characterization must prove the failure before product changes.

The end state is the user's archived local wallet, one confirmed real move, its exact complete allocation pair, and no pending queues after automatic sync cycles. Preserve same-currency decisions, account/session/caller fences, immutable financial facts and every newer mutation. Owned remote archive/tombstone/different intent must hold as a conflict instead of resurrection.
